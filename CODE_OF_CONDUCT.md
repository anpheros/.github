# Code of conduct

We want the Anpheros community to be a respectful, welcoming place for everyone who builds healthcare software —
regardless of background, experience, identity or opinion.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep discussions about the problem, not the person.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Protect privacy: never share real patient data, credentials or other people's personal information.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, deliberate disruption or misuse of the project's spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation for behaviour that breaks this code.
To report a problem, write to contact@anpheros.com; reports are handled confidentially.
